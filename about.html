Medicine Checker

Medicine Checker is a medicine inventory and sales management system designed to make medicine stock management simple, organized and efficient.

About the System

This system helps manage medicines, monitor stock availability, record medicine sales and review sales information from one convenient dashboard. Medicine stock is automatically updated when a sale is completed.

Key Features

  • ✓ Medicine inventory management
  • ✓ Stock availability monitoring
  • ✓ Medicine sales recording
  • ✓ Automatic stock reduction
  • ✓ Sales history and reports
  • ✓ Medicine search
  • ✓ Mobile responsive interface
